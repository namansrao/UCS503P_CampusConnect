## Contribution summary
The database explanation connects the product experience to persistent application state. Profiles, societies, events, registrations, matches, messages, and moderation records remain related through keys instead of being treated as isolated screens. Mutual-interest matching depends on reciprocal likes, while event registration depends on event capacity and registration state.

## Database pitch
The database is more than storage: foreign keys and unique constraints protect relationships, triggers enforce critical invariants, and transactions make multi-step actions operate consistently. The matching query combines shared interests and event overlap while excluding blocked users and previously processed candidates.
